Before you ask

Questions

Who is this for?

Young adults, 18 and older. This year's retreat is for that age group only.

Can I bring a friend from another church?

Yes. Churches across Southern California are invited. Bring anyone 18 or older.

What if I can't pay all $250 right now?

A deposit option is coming. If cost is stopping you, email us or talk to someone on the team.

When do I arrive and leave?

Check-in opens at 4:00 pm on Friday, Dec 18. Pack out Monday morning, Dec 21.

What should I bring?

A packing list and consent form will be emailed after you register. It gets cold in the mountains, so bring layers.
